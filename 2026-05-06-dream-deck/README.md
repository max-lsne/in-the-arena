# DreamDeck

**See the patterns inside your sleep.**

DreamDeck is an AI-powered dream journal that surfaces recurring symbols, themes, and emotional patterns from your dreams over time. Capture fragments by voice the moment you wake up, and watch a personal symbol graph emerge across weeks and months.

## The idea

Most dream journaling apps are glorified note-taking. The interesting signal isn't a single dream — it's the **patterns across hundreds of dreams**: which symbols recur, which people haunt your sleep during stressful periods, how the emotional weather of your nights tracks against your waking life.

DreamDeck is built around three insights:

1. **Capture has to be friction-free.** Voice-first, mumble-tolerant, works with eyes closed.
2. **The value is the graph, not the entry.** Symbols, characters, settings, and moods are tagged automatically and linked across time.
3. **Privacy is non-negotiable.** Dreams are the most intimate data a person produces. End-to-end encrypted by default.

## What's in this folder

A static landing page demonstrating the product positioning, with a small interactive demo that performs lightweight symbol "readings" entirely client-side.

- `index.html` — landing page structure
- `styles.css` — visual design (aurora background, glass cards, dark mode)
- `script.js` — waitlist form + interactive symbol-reading demo

## Run locally

Just open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
