# Backbone

**Practice the conversation that scares you. Then have it.**

Backbone is an AI voice coach for the conversations you keep avoiding —
asking for a raise, telling a report their work isn't there yet, saying no
to a project, re-pricing a client, opening the hard talk with a co-founder.
You rehearse with an AI that plays the other side and pushes back the way
real people do. You leave with a scorecard, a posture, and one line to
take into the real room.

## The pitch in one breath

You don't rise to the level of your courage. You fall to the level of your
rehearsals. Backbone is the rehearsal.

## The six rooms

- **The Raise** — the ask, the silence, the four pushbacks.
- **The Honest Review** — the sentence you've been rewriting for three weeks.
- **The No** — declining without the apology spiral.
- **The Split** — equity, roles, or "we want different things" with a co-founder.
- **The Re-price** — telling a client your rate is going up.
- **The Boundary** — the personal one. The script you owe yourself.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page and the typed rehearsal demo.
- `styles.css` — paper / spine / rust palette; Fraunces, Inter, JetBrains Mono.
- `script.js` — pick a room, set difficulty, type your opening line. Get a
  mocked pushback, a Backbone score across four axes (posture, specifics,
  silence, recovery), three lines that landed, three to fix, and a take-home
  line for the real room. Everything runs in the browser.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
