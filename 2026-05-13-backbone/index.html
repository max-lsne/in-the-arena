How a rehearsal goes.

  1. 01

    Brief the room

    Three sentences. Who you're talking to, what you want, and what you're afraid they'll say. Backbone uses it to build the other side.

  2. 02

    Talk it through — out loud

    You speak. The AI answers in voice, in character, with the difficulty dialed where you set it. It pushes back, dodges, and goes quiet — like real people do.

  3. 03

    Get the scorecard

    Posture, specificity, silence, recovery. Three lines you nailed, three you flinched on, one line to take into the real room.
