# Headland

**Where the land looks ahead.**

A headland is the piece of land that juts out farthest into open
water. It is the spot a coast keeps for the work of looking — for
seeing the weather and the boats and whatever else is coming in
long before the rest of the shore does. Headland is a small page
for that work: the squalls on the horizon, the sails making for
the harbor, and the flotsam drifting in on the tide.

## The pitch in one breath

A backlog tells you what you have not done. A calendar tells you
what you have agreed to. Neither one tells you what is coming
toward you on its own — the storm two days out, the visitor whose
boat is still a speck, the thing the tide is bringing in whether
you wanted it or not. Headland is a page that asks you to stand at
the point, look outward, and name them.

## The horizon

- **Squalls** — the weather you can see arriving. A storm, a
  deadline, a difficult conversation. You did not call for it. You
  can see it before it sees you.
- **Sails** — the boats making for harbor. A visitor, a launch, a
  return, a yes you are waiting on. Something with intent.
  Something that wants to land.
- **Flotsam** — the small things drifting in on the tide. Not
  storms, not boats. Newsletters, requests, half-formed asks, the
  next round of small unsolicited noise.

## The four states of a sighting

- **Sighted** — just visible. A shape on the edge of the water.
  Named, not yet near.
- **Closing** — close enough to count. The shape is the thing now;
  it will land soon if nothing changes.
- **Landed** — it arrived. The storm came in, the boat tied up,
  the flotsam washed ashore. Now it is a thing on the beach.
- **Passed** — it went by without contact. The storm slid south.
  The boat made for the next harbor. The flotsam drifted out
  again. Worth remembering: most things pass.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page with a horizon view in the
  browser.
- `styles.css` — a coastal palette: foam, fog, slate, gull, kelp,
  cedar, salt-sun; Fraunces for headings, Inter for body,
  JetBrains Mono for marginalia.
- `script.js` — ten seeded sightings for an imaginary first week
  of June 2026 at a small design studio on a quiet coast. The
  sails lean toward a Friday visit from a long-quiet client; the
  squalls and flotsam lean around it.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
