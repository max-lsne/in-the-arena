# Millrace

**A small page for the channel that powers the work.**

A millrace is the channeled length of water that brings the stream to
the mill wheel. It is not the river. It is not the wheel. It is the
small straight cut between them — half a mile of dressed stone and a
patient slope, one inch in twenty, all the way to the paddle. The river
runs anywhere and turns no wheel. The water in the race is already
chosen.

## The pitch in one breath

Most productivity asks where to find more water. The river is wide and
plentiful and powers nothing. The work is not in finding more water;
the work is in cutting a narrow channel and keeping it clear. A
millrace is the practice of having one small straight cut for each
piece of long work, and trusting the slope to do the turning.

## The four cuts

- **Trickle** — a finger's width of water, enough for a hand quern: a
  daily note, a half-hour walk, the short letter. Easy to dig. Easy to
  forget you dug it.
- **Leat** — a short upland channel of bare stone: a chapter to be
  drafted, an instrument practised, a garden corner cleared. Runs
  straight and quiet without fuss.
- **Race** — the full dressed-stone millrace: a year-long work, a
  novel, a slow recovery, a course finished evening by evening. Cut
  once and kept clear.
- **Penstock** — the high-pressure pipe down the mountain: the
  decade-long works, the rebuilt instrument, the second career. The
  pressure is the same patient inch of slope, multiplied by many
  miles.

## The four states of a channel

- **At the spring** — the water is still in the wide river. Not yet
  drawn off, not yet committed to a direction.
- **In the head** — the water is held above the wheel. Already chosen,
  gathered, brimming, but not yet falling on a paddle.
- **Running the race** — the sluice is open, the water is on the
  paddles, the wheel is turning. Nothing dramatic. The race is full.
- **Tailrace** — the water has done its turn and runs out under the
  mill, back to the river. The day's work, done, flows downstream.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page, with a small spring, a small race,
  a small overshot wheel turning patiently against the side of a small
  stone mill.
- `styles.css` — a millrace palette: limestone, mist, water, water-deep,
  moss, hemp, copper-glint, mill-wood, shadow, ink; Fraunces for
  headings, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for the milepost marks.
- `script.js` — twelve seeded channels cut by no one in particular
  along a slow turn of the spring of 2026. A proof carried one lemma
  at a time, an instrument carved a belly a month, a quartet drafted
  sixteen bars a week, a fifteen-year symphony whose head is not yet
  half full.
