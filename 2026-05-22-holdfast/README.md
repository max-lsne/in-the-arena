# Holdfast

**The small grip that keeps the wave from carrying you off.**

Kelp does not have roots. It has a holdfast — a small, patient
grip on the bedrock. The grip does not feed the kelp; it only
keeps the tide from carrying it away. Holdfast is a small page
for the few practices that do that for you.

## The pitch in one breath

Habit apps want a streak. The streak is an arrow: a long line
going up. The arrow punishes you for the day you almost did not
hold. A holdfast is not an arrow. It is a grip on the rock. Some
of yours have held for years. Some are new and slip half the
time. Some you have loosed on purpose. The bed is the practice.

## The three tides of a grip

- **Tide-in** — the morning grip. The first thing, before the
  day fills up.
- **Slack** — the midday grip. A small grip when the water sits
  still and the work is hardest.
- **Tide-out** — the evening grip. The one that holds through
  the night and into tomorrow.

## The four states of a grip

- **Drift** — you have named it. It has not gripped today.
- **Hold** — it has gripped today.
- **Anchor** — it has held long enough that you barely remember
  starting.
- **Loosed** — it slipped, and you took it off the rock on
  purpose. Loosed is honest.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page, with the bed widget in the
  browser.
- `styles.css` — a sea-glass palette: kelp, tidepool, foam, sand,
  shell, wet-stone, amber; Fraunces for headings, Inter for body,
  JetBrains Mono for marginalia.
- `script.js` — twelve seeded grips kept by no one in particular
  through the slow spring of 2026. The bed: a kettle held seven
  days, a notebook held seven months, a candle held nearly a
  year, a card-writing slack grip loosed on purpose in March.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
