Questions

Do I have to scan everything I own?

No. Scan a shelf, a grocery bag, or just type a few staples. The model fills gaps and asks when it's unsure.

What about dietary restrictions?

Set allergens, diet (vegan, halal, keto, etc.), and dislikes once. PantryPilot will never suggest something you can't eat.

Which grocers integrate?

At launch: Instacart, Amazon Fresh, Walmart, Tesco, Ocado, and Whole Foods. More coming weekly.

Is my data sold?

Never. We make money from subscriptions, not your shopping list. Your pantry data stays yours and is encrypted at rest.
