# Holloway

**A small page for the lane worn deep by patient feet.**

A holloway is a sunken way — a lane cut down into the soft sandstone or
chalk of the countryside by the slow, repeated passage of feet, hooves,
and cartwheels over centuries. No one decides to dig a holloway. It is
not a road that was built. It is a road that was *walked* — and walked,
and walked, until the ground gave way underfoot and the path settled
quietly into the hillside, head-deep in places, with the hedges meeting
in a green vault overhead.

## The pitch in one breath

Most days the work is a field — wide open, no path through it, every
morning a fresh decision about where to set the foot. The holloway is
the other thing. A line you have walked enough times that the ground
remembers it. Once it is worn in, you don't choose anymore. You step
into the lane and it carries you where it has always carried you,
between banks that the centuries have made.

## The four cuts

- **Trace** — the faint dimming of grass along a line a foot wide:
  the daily entry, the half-page note, the walk to the gate at first
  light. Always there. Always trodden. Never sunk an inch.
- **Hollow** — an ankle-deep groove with a rounded floor: the weekly
  errand, the Saturday lesson, the chapter under second revision.
  Visibly worn. Has begun, faintly, to gather its own banks.
- **Sunken** — a lane knee- to shoulder-deep, banked with fern and
  ivy and root: the long course taken evening by evening, the book
  in second draft, the practice carried twenty years.
- **Cathedral** — head-deep and over, the hedgerows meeting in a
  vault above, a green tunnel cool in August, the cart-ruts of a
  century beneath the leaf-mould: a generational work, a parish
  road, a thing the village walks without remembering who began.

## The four states of a lane

- **Untrodden today** — the lane is at rest between walks, the soil
  holding the print of the last foot, the air still under the
  hedges. The path is full of itself.
- **Walked** — the lane has been used this morning. The litter is
  pressed. A bramble swings back into place. The dust has the soft
  print of the heel.
- **Worn deeper** — the lane is going down a little this season,
  cart-rut a little wider, the bank a little higher to either side.
  The wear is happening.
- **Closed in** — the lane has not been walked in a while. Nettles
  are up to the knee, brambles arching across, a small ash come up
  in the middle. The lane is being given back.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page, with a small holloway between two
  banks at the edge of a wood, the hedgerows meeting overhead, a
  long shaft of low afternoon light reaching down to the lane floor.
- `styles.css` — a holloway palette: loam, sandstone, chalk, fern,
  moss, hedge, holly, ochre, root, shadow, ink; Fraunces for
  headings, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for the milestone marks
  along the bank.
- `script.js` — twelve lanes seeded along a parish in the middle of
  June 2026. A drovers' road over the down at the cathedral cut, a
  pasture-gate trace walked since 1903, a half-mile sunken way from
  the farmhouse to the hayfield that has gathered four feet of bank
  in eighty years.
