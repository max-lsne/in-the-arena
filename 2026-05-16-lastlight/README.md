# Lastlight

**A tender list for the last weeks of a place.**

Lastlight is a small companion for people in the final weeks of
somewhere — the renter who's leaving the city in October, the student
finishing a year abroad, the friend who got the job in a different
country. You set a leave date. You add the small things you want to
do one more time. The light goes down a little each day. The list is
the goodbye.

## The pitch in one breath

Moving is logistics; leaving is a list. Lastlight keeps the small,
unrepeatable things — the bench, the corner table, the long walk —
where you can still reach them.

## Five kinds of light

- **Walk** — a route, a view, a stretch of river. Somewhere your feet
  already know.
- **Table** — a meal, a coffee, a counter where someone says your name.
- **People** — one person, one short visit, before the city moves
  someone out of reach.
- **Errand** — the small place you kept meaning to try, and now you
  have the excuse.
- **Quiet** — a bench, a stairwell, a roof. Somewhere to just sit and
  notice it once more.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page with the in-browser leaving list.
- `styles.css` — dusk palette: ember, plum, slate, and cream;
  Source Serif 4 for headings, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for
  marginalia.
- `script.js` — twenty-one seeded entries for a fictional last month
  in a small city; mark kept, mark passed, add your own, reset.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
