FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this just another Pomodoro timer?

No. Pomodoros assume your brain works the same at 9am and 3pm. FocusFox watches what actually happens and adapts. Sessions are 20–110 minutes depending on your day, not a fixed 25.

Will you read my calendar / messages?

We read titles and times to plan around them. We never read meeting bodies, attachments, or messages. Everything is encrypted at rest, and you can wipe your account in one click.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The desktop app keeps your plan and timers running offline. It syncs when you're back.

What about ADHD / non-linear days?

FocusFox was built with ADHD makers in our beta. It avoids guilt loops, surfaces tiny next-actions, and never punishes you for skipping a session.

Can I get a refund?

Anytime in the first 30 days. Email us, no forms.
