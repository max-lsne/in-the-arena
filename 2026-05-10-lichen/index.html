How a patch of knowledge grows.

  1. 01

    Install, then forget about it

    Lichen sits in the address bar. It watches your queries the way a notebook watches a pen — quietly, locally, no nudges, no streaks.

  2. 02

    The second time, it asks

    When the same question comes back, Lichen taps you on the shoulder: "Want to keep what you found?" One line, one tag, one save.

  3. 03

    The third time, it's already there

    The next rhyme of that question lands on your own note before the search engine even loads. Slowly the patch fills in.
