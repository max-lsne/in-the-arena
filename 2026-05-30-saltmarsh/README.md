# Saltmarsh

**A slow ledger of the things that settle between the tides.**

A saltmarsh is the strip of coast that is neither sea nor land — flooded
twice a day by the tide and yet, on the slow turn of decades, the most
patient land on the planet. It holds carbon. It dulls the storm. It
catches the wrack the sea no longer wants and, in a hundred years,
turns it into ground. Saltmarsh is a small page for the few things in
a life that work the same way: caught at the edge, held quietly,
layered for a long time.

## The pitch in one breath

Most ledgers want a column going up. A saltmarsh adds a millimetre a
year — a third of an inch in a decade, a foot in a century, a few feet
in a lifetime. It is the shape of practice that lasts a long time. The
tide is not interruption. The tide is the work.

## The three zones

- **Pan** — the still inner deposits of the self: the morning silence,
  the cold-water swim, the practice that does not leave the marsh.
- **Channel** — the working flow: the projects you let the tide work
  on, the slow newsletters, the open notebooks of a craft.
- **Wrack** — what arrives at the edge from elsewhere: the Tuesday
  call, the long walk with a parent, the letter habit to a person far
  away.

## The four states of a deposit

- **Drift** — named, but not yet caught. The thing that floats past
  every spring tide and is not held.
- **Catch** — first holding. Surface-snagged at the wrack line.
  Fragile. The first month or two.
- **Hold** — rooted in the sward. The cordgrass has it. It will not
  wash away with the next storm.
- **Layer** — long-settled, deep in the mud. Years of holding, slowly
  becoming ground.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page, with the marsh widget in the
  browser.
- `styles.css` — a saltmarsh palette: spartina, samphire, glasswort,
  mud, silt, tidal blue, paper salt; Fraunces for headings, Inter for
  body, JetBrains Mono for marginalia.
- `script.js` — twelve seeded deposits kept by no one in particular
  through a slow turn of the spring of 2026. The marsh: a morning
  silence layered eight years, a cold-water swim just caught, a slow
  database project held six, a Tuesday tea on the wrack line of twelve.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
