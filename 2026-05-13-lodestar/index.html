You don't have a knowledge problem.
You have a loading problem.

The principle you needed on Tuesday was somewhere — in a Notes app, a Slack DM to yourself, the margin of a book three moves ago. You made the decision without it. A week later, you remembered.

Lodestar is the smallest possible fix: one place for the principles you've actually earned, surfaced once a day, and one tap away when the room gets heavy.

The loop

  1. 01

    Capture

    Add a principle the day you earn it. One sentence. The version that survives stress, not the inspirational one.

  2. 02

    Anchor

    Each morning, Lodestar surfaces one. You read it once. Notification done. That's the entire daily ritual.

  3. 03

    Use

    When a hard decision shows up, tag it to the principle that should have guided it. Even before the call is made.

  4. 04

    Audit

    Each quarter, see which principles actually fire and which never do. Sharpen the live ones. Retire the dead ones.
