ripple · noun · /ˈrɪp.əl/ — a small, regular rise and fall riding on a steady flow; here the twice-a-turn surge and drag of a joint's output, a flutter that comes back on every revolution it cannot smooth away

Ripple

The corner does not just shift the turn. It makes the turn breathe.

The uneven angle of a universal joint has a second face. If the output is always a little ahead, then behind, then ahead again, then its speed must run fast, then slow, then fast — because position is just speed added up. Differentiate the joint's law and out comes the velocity ratio, ω₂/ω₁ = cos β / (1 − sin²β·cos²θ): the output turns at up to 1/cos β of the input rate when the driving yoke lies in the plane of the bend, and as little as cos β a quarter-turn later. It swings twice for every single turn of the shaft — a second harmonic, the same lopsided breathing a short connecting rod gives a piston. Flatten the bend and the breath goes shallow; steepen it, and the shaft shudders. And since it is a flutter of speed, it is felt harder the faster you run — which is the whole reason a single joint is never trusted with a fast, steep drive.

Why the speed swings twice

From an uneven angle to a breathing speed

  • Speed is the slope of position. Take tan φ = tan θ / cos β and differentiate: ω₂/ω₁ = cos β / (1 − sin²β·cos²θ). The output rate is never the input rate except where the two happen to cross.
  • The denominator carries cos²θ, which repeats every half-turn — so the ratio repeats twice per revolution. One turn of the shaft, two full breaths of the output. That doubling is what makes it a second harmonic.
  • It is fastest, 1/cos β, at θ = 0, 180°, where the driving yoke lies in the plane of the two shafts; slowest, cos β, at θ = 90, 270°, square to it. The two extremes are exact reciprocals — the output borrows speed and pays it back.
  • The depth is all the bend's: at 10° the swing is about ±1.5%, at 30° near ±15%, at 45° fully ±41%. A gentle corner barely stirs; a steep one heaves.
  • Because it is a flutter of rate, its real violence scales with speed: the accelerations it demands go as the square of the rpm. That is the hum and the wear — and the reason the error is not endured but cancelled.

A single joint keeps honest books over the turn and cheats on the beat: every revolution it hands back exactly what it took, and every instant between, it is either in debt or in credit.
