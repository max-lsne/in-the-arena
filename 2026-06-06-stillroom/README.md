# Stillroom

**A small page for the slow preparations.**

The stillroom was the quiet room of the old house — between the kitchen
and the cellar — where preserves were laid down, tinctures distilled,
soaps cured, herbs hung to dry, vinegars steeped. The work was patient
and unhurried, the pay-off months or years out. The mistress of the
stillroom did not measure her day by what she finished; she measured it
by what she had put down to wait. Stillroom is a small page for the same
shape of work in a modern life.

## The pitch in one breath

Most productivity counts what you ship today. The stillroom counts what
you put down today to be ready next winter. A jar of damson gin doesn't
help in September; the September jar helps in February. The stillroom is
the practice of laying things down on purpose — slow letters, slow
reading, slow research, slow rest — knowing they will not return what
they cost for a long while, and not minding.

## The four shelves

- **Pickling** — things kept by brine and acid: the small commitments
  preserved against the rush (a Friday close-out, an inbox-zero hour,
  a once-a-week tidy of the desk).
- **Drying** — things kept by removing the wet of haste: the reading
  set down to be returned to slowly (a long book a season at a time,
  a notebook one walks back through, an essay let to age).
- **Distilling** — things reduced down to their essence by patience and
  low heat: a year of journals into a single page, a long conversation
  into one good question, a thousand reads into a short shelf.
- **Curing** — things that need time and stillness to become themselves:
  a project let to rest before launch, a friendship not pushed, a grief
  given its full season, a soap left on the rack twelve weeks.

## The four states of a jar

- **Laid down** — newly put up. Sealed, labelled, dated. Nothing to do
  but wait.
- **Settling** — the first weeks. The brine finds its level, the
  alcohol takes the fruit, the soap firms in the mold.
- **Ready** — opened-able. Will keep getting better, but the door is
  unlatched now.
- **Long-kept** — a year or more held. The flavour has gone where only
  patience can carry it.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page, with the stillroom shelves in the
  browser.
- `styles.css` — a stillroom palette: parchment, beeswax, bottle-glass,
  brine, copper, camphor, elderberry, oakgall; Fraunces for headings,
  Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for the labels on the jars.
- `script.js` — twelve seeded jars kept by no one in particular through
  a slow autumn of 2026. The shelves: a damson gin laid down nine
  Septembers, a Friday close-out cured eleven years against the same
  desk, a year of journals distilled to a single index card every
  midwinter.
