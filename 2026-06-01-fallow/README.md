# Fallow

**A small page for the work you have set down on purpose, so it can come back better.**

A fallow is a field deliberately left unsown for a season — a year, sometimes
two, sometimes seven — so the soil can recover from being worked. The
fallow is not idle ground. It is the practice that keeps the field worth
sowing again. The Romans rotated thirds. The medieval English rotated
halves. The longest-kept fields in the world have always been the ones
that were also, regularly, the longest-rested. Fallow is a small page
for the few things in a working life that work the same way.

## The pitch in one breath

Most planners want every field worked every season — every project
shipping, every newsletter weekly, every notebook open. A fallow is a
quiet field on purpose. It is the slowest shape in the working year,
and the most patient — and when the next sowing comes, the soil is
deeper for the rest. The rest is not interruption. The rest is the work.

## The three lands

- **Field** — the central work you have set down: a book between drafts,
  a company in the year after sale, a research line waiting on its slow
  data.
- **Margin** — the smaller things at the edges, set down on purpose: a
  newsletter rested for a winter, a photo feed left to grow over, the
  conference circuit you stopped traveling for.
- **Hedgerow** — the boundary practices you keep year-round to mark
  what's resting: a Sunday with no work, the once-a-quarter check-in,
  the long walk where the field is in sight but you do not enter it.

## The four states of a fallow

- **Standing** — the field is still being worked. Not yet set down. You
  mean to rest it, and you have not.
- **Set down** — the cut just happened. The field is bare. Fragile;
  easy to plough back in a panic.
- **Resting** — deep in the fallow year. The soil is restoring. The
  hedges are growing in. You are not the one doing the work.
- **Sward** — the long-fallow ground. Years of rest. A deep mature
  cover that has become its own quiet thing. Some fields stay this way.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page, with the fallow widget in the
  browser.
- `styles.css` — a fallow palette: straw, ochre, loam, clay, hedgerow
  green, moss, bracken, chalk; Fraunces for headings, Inter for body,
  JetBrains Mono for marginalia.
- `script.js` — twelve seeded fallows kept by no one in particular
  through a slow turn of the spring of 2026. The land: a second book
  set down a year, a small company resting four, a photo feed gone
  sward, a Sunday with no work standing eleven years uncut.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
