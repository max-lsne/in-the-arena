How a run down the ramp works.

  1. 01

    Write your one-pager

    One sentence of what it does, one paragraph of who it's for, a price, and a screenshot if you have one. Fifteen minutes, max.

  2. 02

    It drops to 50 builders

    Each one scores it on a single question — "would I pay this?" — and writes two lines of why. They don't see each other's answers.

  3. 03

    You get one number, three sentences

    Forty-eight hours later: a Would-Pay Score, three distilled takeaways, and a recommendation. Commit, pivot, or kill — but decide.
