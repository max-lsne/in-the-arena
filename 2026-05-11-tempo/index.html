How it works

A calmer day, in three steps.

No more dragging blocks around your calendar at 11pm. Tempo learns, plans, and adapts — quietly in the background.

01

Connect your signals

Calendar, sleep tracker, focus sessions, even your typing cadence. Tempo turns scattered data into one energy curve.

02

Learn your rhythm

After ~10 days, Tempo knows your peaks, dips, and recovery windows — and how meetings, exercise, and caffeine shift them.

03

Plan tomorrow, tonight

Each evening, Tempo drafts your next day. Approve in one tap, or tweak — it learns from every change you make.

Features

The scheduler that respects your biology.

Peak window detection

Tempo finds your two daily focus peaks and reserves them for the work that actually matters.

Meeting auto-shuffle

Suggests respectful reschedules so collaboration lands in your social hours, not your deep work.

Recovery prompts

Micro-breaks, light exposure, and hydration nudges timed to the moment your attention dips.

Sleep-aware planning

Bad night? Tempo softens tomorrow — fewer decisions, lighter cognitive load, gentler start.

Team mode

Find overlap windows where everyone is at their best. Stop scheduling 8am brainstorms with night owls.

Privacy-first

Health signals stay on device. Tempo only sees the patterns, not the raw data — and never sells either.

The science

Your body has a schedule. Most apps ignore it.

Cognitive performance can swing up to 26% between your peak and trough hours. Most people spend their best hours in meetings and their worst ones trying to think hard.

Tempo is built on chronobiology research from Stanford, the Karolinska Institute, and 40+ years of shift-work science — then personalized to one person: you.
