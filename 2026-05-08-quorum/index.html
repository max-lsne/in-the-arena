A calm room for group decisions.

Decide together, without the group chat spiral.

Quorum is a tiny app for groups who can't agree on where to eat, what to watch, or when to meet. Everyone votes privately, the app surfaces overlap, and the "what do you want?" loop ends in under a minute.

Three steps from "I don't know" to "okay, let's go".

  1. 01

    Open a room

    One question, one link. "Where to eat?" "What to watch?" "When to meet?" Anyone with the link can join.

  2. 02

    Drop options, vote in private

    Anyone can add an option. Each person rates them 1–5 alone — no live tally, no avatars next to scores.

  3. 03

    Reveal the overlap

    Quorum picks the highest floor: the option with the best worst-score. The thing everyone can live with.
