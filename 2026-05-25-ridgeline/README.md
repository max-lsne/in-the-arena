# Ridgeline

**The line where the project tips.**

A ridgeline is the long high path along the top of a range. From
the ridge you can see both valleys. The decisions you make on it
are the ones that drop the project to one side or the other.
Ridgeline is a small page for the week's ridge — the summits, the
saddles, and the approaches that lead to them.

## The pitch in one breath

A backlog is a flat list and a roadmap is a line on a map. Neither
of them knows where the ridge is. Most weeks have one summit that,
once crossed, sends the project down one valley and not the other,
and a handful of saddles that look small from below and turn out
to be the part you remember. Ridgeline is a page that asks for the
ridge, not the list.

## The ridge

- **The summit** — the one decision the week is built around. From
  this point the project drops one way and not the other. Named
  first. Crossed last.
- **The saddles** — the low passes between summits. Smaller calls
  that give the ridge its shape. Approachable from either side.
- **The approaches** — the climbs. The work that gets you near
  enough to a summit to see it for what it is.

## The four states of a crossing

- **Sighted** — you can see the summit ahead. Named, not approached.
- **Crossed** — the call is made and the ridge is behind you.
- **Skirted** — you went around instead of over. Honest if you
  meant it; less so if you didn't.
- **Returned** — you came back over the same point. Some ridges
  are walked twice on purpose.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page with the ridge profile in the
  browser.
- `styles.css` — a highland palette: cloud, granite, slate, lichen,
  heather, mist, alpenglow; Fraunces for headings, Inter for body,
  JetBrains Mono for marginalia.
- `script.js` — nine seeded crossings for an imaginary launch week
  in late May 2026. The summit is shipping to ten people the week
  before the rest of the world sees it. The saddles and approaches
  lean toward that one crossing.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
