# Sounding

**A weekly note from where the work is, deep down.**

Sounding is a small page for the long project you're already inside.
Once a week — Sunday is a good day for it — you take a sounding.
Five quick questions, scored low to high. The sum is the week's
depth. Across months, the line draws the seabed of the work:
the shallow weeks, the deep ones, the storms when the bottom
fell away.

## The pitch in one breath

Streak apps want a flat staircase up. Sounding wants the truth of
the floor — how deep you went this week, what the bottom felt
like, and whether you can still see the next step. One sounding
a week. The chart is the year.

## The five facets

- **Bearing** — how clearly can I see the next step?
- **Reader** — how well do I know who it's for?
- **Fit** — how well does this week's work sit on last week's?
- **Instinct** — how much of it was muscle, not calculation?
- **Hold** — how much of the time was it the only thing in my head?

Each facet is scored one to five. The sum, out of twenty-five, is
the week's sounding.

## And the bottom

After the score, a single word for what the floor felt like:

- **Sand** — easy, found, settled.
- **Rock** — hard but holding. Deep work that did not slip.
- **Kelp** — tangled. A week of pulling at knots.
- **No bottom** — the line did not catch. A storm week, or
  one waiting on something outside the work.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page with the in-browser chart.
- `styles.css` — a chart-paper palette: hadal, photic, surface,
  warm parchment, faint rust; Fraunces for headings, Inter for
  body, JetBrains Mono for marginalia.
- `script.js` — twenty seeded soundings from a fictional
  five-month build of a single writing desk by hand; take your
  own, change a facet, edit the bottom, reset.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
