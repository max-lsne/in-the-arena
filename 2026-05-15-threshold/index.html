Most apps reward doing.
This one notices what you are not doing.

The conversations we never start are not on any to-do list, because to-do lists are about tasks, and these are not tasks. They are thresholds. They sit in a corner of the house we walk past every day.

Threshold is the only list that grows when you ignore it. The longer a doorway sits unopened, the more it weighs. Not to shame you — to let you see, plainly, what the inside of your year actually looks like.

House rules

  1. 01

    Six doorways, no more

    If a seventh one matters more than one of the six, swap. The cap is not a feature — it's the whole product.

  2. 02

    One sentence, plain

    "Tell Mum I'm not coming for Christmas." Not a plan. Not the script. The honest sentence that names the doorway.

  3. 03

    The weight rises on its own

    Every day a doorway sits, it gains a little. You don't decide its weight. The calendar does.

  4. 04

    "Had" is the only verb

    Not "done." Not "completed." When the conversation has happened — well, badly, partly — you mark it had. The doorway closes. The list breathes.

The six rooms

  • Family. The ones you grew up around. The ones, increasingly, who grew up around you.
  • Work. The cofounder. The hire. The boss. The thing on the agenda that never quite makes it.
  • Money. The rent talk. The salary ask. The number on the page neither of you has said out loud.
  • Body. The doctor. The therapist. The phone call you have been almost-making for two months.
  • Friends. The repair. The apology. The "are we still close, or is this politeness now."
  • Self. The drinking. The job. The move. The thing only you and the ceiling at 3am know about.

Who keeps a threshold

The first-time founder

The cofounder conversation is the one. It has been waiting since February. Threshold doesn't tell you to have it. It just stops letting you pretend it isn't there.

The eldest child

The Christmas one. The "I'm not the one who organises this anymore" one. Threshold lets you keep it without it leaking onto every dinner you have until then.

The almost-quiet drinker

The one with yourself. It has been on the list for two years under three different sentences. Threshold lets the sentence change, but not the doorway.

The friend who moved away

The repair with the friend you stopped writing to. It is not urgent. It is just heavy. Threshold weighs it honestly, and that is most of the help.
