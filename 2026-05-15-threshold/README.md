# Threshold

**The conversations you keep meaning to have.**

Threshold is a quiet, private list of the unstarted conversations in
your life — with your sister, your cofounder, your landlord, yourself.
You don't write the dialogue. You just stop pretending the doorway
isn't there.

## The pitch in one breath

Most apps reward doing. This one notices what you are not doing — and
makes the weight of it visible, without making a scene.

## Six rooms

- **Family** — the ones you grew up around. The ones, increasingly,
  who grew up around you.
- **Work** — the cofounder, the hire, the boss. The thing on the
  agenda that never quite makes it.
- **Money** — the rent talk, the salary ask, the number on the page
  neither of you has said out loud.
- **Body** — the doctor, the therapist, the phone call you've been
  almost-making for two months.
- **Friends** — the repair, the apology, the "are we still close."
- **Self** — the drinking, the job, the move. The one only you and
  the ceiling at 3am know about.

## House rules

1. Six doorways, no more. The cap is the whole product.
2. One sentence per doorway, plain.
3. The weight rises on its own — every day a doorway sits, it gains
   a little.
4. "Had" is the only verb. Not done. Not completed.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — landing page with the in-browser doorways demo.
- `styles.css` — lamplight cream, ember, and slate; Fraunces for
  headings, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for marginalia.
- `script.js` — six seeded doorways from a thirty-four-year-old
  founder's mid-May; retag the room, change the waiting, mark one
  had, reset. State persists in `localStorage`.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
