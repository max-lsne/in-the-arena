# Fathom

**A small page for the few texts you mean to understand to the bottom.**

A fathom is six feet of still water, and the only way to know how deep
you stand is to drop a line for it. The old way was a lead weight on a
marked rope, its foot hollowed and packed with tallow, paid out hand
over hand until it touched — a sounding. You read the depth off the
knots, and you read the ground off the tallow: shell, sand, soft mud,
clean rock, whatever the bottom pressed up into the grease. A library
is the same. It is not the count of spines your eye has crossed. It is
the few texts you have dropped a real line into, again and again, until
you know what the bottom is made of.

## The pitch in one breath

The count is a lie that flatters — fifty-two books a year, a shelf that
fills, a number that goes up, and not one of them has changed how you
think because you skated the surface of every one. Depth is the other
measure: a handful of texts you sound season after season until you
know not just what they say but what they are made of at the bottom.
You cannot do it to fifty things at once. You choose a few, and you go
down.

## The four levels (depth reached)

- **Surface** — read once, skated over, only the top of it known. The
  article finished on the train, the book you can say you have read.
  The line touched the meniscus and went no further.
- **Shallows** — reread, the shape and lie of it known. You have been
  down a few times and can chart the run of it. Real acquaintance now,
  but not yet the bottom.
- **Reach** — worked through and argued with, notes crowding the
  margin. You have paid out real line — taken it apart, fought it,
  carried it around for weeks. Deep water, and the lead is finding ground.
- **Deep** — lived with for years, sounded to the bottom, it has
  changed how you think. You know what the lead brings up before it
  surfaces. The few texts that become part of how you see.

## The four states (condition)

- **Adrift** — not yet sounded, drifting over it. Bought with intent,
  opened once or not at all, the line still coiled on the thwart.
- **Sounding** — taking the depth now, actively in it. The line is
  paying out, the lead falling, the work under way.
- **Holding** — anchored over it, returning to it on a line. You come
  back to it on a course you have already marked.
- **Fathomed** — understood to the bottom; you know what the lead
  brings up. The work is not finished, but the ground is known.

## The metric

**Soundings** — how many times you have gone back down into a text.
Not "read": "not yet sounded", "1 sounding", "12 soundings". A text
comes into condition as the soundings accrue.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the landing page, with a sounding in progress: a
  boat's waterline at top-left, a brass lead-line dropping through
  layered bands of water from pale shallows to dark deep, mark-red
  depth-marks on the line, the tallow-footed lead just touching a soft
  mud bottom, a faint charted contour behind it.
- `styles.css` — a sea-chart palette: pale chart-buff parchment, deep
  navy-teal sea, lead grey, the brass of the sounding-line, tallow
  cream, mark-red. Fraunces for the headings, Inter for the body,
  JetBrains Mono for the soundings.
- `script.js` — twelve texts across a fictional reader's chart-table in
  the middle of June 2026. A proof reread for fifteen years and finally
  fathomed, a poem in translation forty drafts deep, a paper skated
  once on the train, a late quartet read at the piano each winter, one
  psalm said eleven thousand times.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
