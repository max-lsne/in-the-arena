# Confluence

**Three small pages, made the same morning, for the same idea seen from three sides:
the things that only hold if you don't hurry them.**

Most days in this folder are one page. Some mornings the idea comes in
threes. A confluence is where streams that started in different combes,
on different hills, in different weather, come down and meet and run on
as one water. These three started apart — a dune, a sounding-line, a
brine-crock — and they meet here, because they are all saying the one
thing in three trades.

## The three

- **[Marram](./marram/)** — *binding what drifts.* You cannot hold a
  shifting dune by standing on it. You plant marram grass, a tuft at a
  time, and wait for it to send its runners down and knit the sand into
  ground that holds. A small page for the fragile new habit that will
  blow away by Thursday unless something roots it first.
  Seed → Tuft → Sward → Dune. Loose → Rooting → Knitting → Bound.

- **[Fathom](./fathom/)** — *understanding by depth, not by count.* A
  library is not how many books you have passed your eyes over; it is
  how deep you have gone in a few. Sailors took the depth with a
  weighted line and tallow on its foot, knot by knot, a sounding at a
  time. A small page for the handful of texts you mean to understand to
  the bottom, not the hundred you mean to have read.
  Surface → Shallows → Reach → Deep. Adrift → Sounding → Holding → Fathomed.

- **[Brine](./brine/)** — *keeping what you don't act on green.* Not
  every idea should be eaten the day it is cut. Some you put down in
  salt, weight under a stone, and forget in the cool dark; you go back
  and turn them, skim the bloom, and one winter they have cured into
  something that keeps for years. A small page for the raw, unripe
  notion that is better soured slow than acted on fresh.
  Raw → Crock → Cellar → Larder. Fresh → Salting → Souring → Cured.

## The shared shape

Each page is the same small machine the folder keeps building: a hero,
the problem stated plainly, four steps, an interactive board of a dozen
seeded entries you can sort, advance, and reset, four people who keep
one of these for real, and a quiet waitlist that promises one email and
no streaks. Four levels, four states, a number that only goes up if you
keep coming back. No build step — open any `index.html` in a browser.
