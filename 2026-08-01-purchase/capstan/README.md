# Capstan

**Wrap a rope a few turns round a drum and the friction does the holding. Your
hand only keeps the tail from paying out — and the hold you need falls away
faster than half with every turn.**

A *capstan* is just a drum — a bollard, a windlass barrel, a winch, a bitt. Its
magic is not that it turns but that a rope wound round it grips it, and the grip
compounds. Where the rope first touches, it carries the load's full pull; as it
wraps, the drum's friction bleeds that pull away, so by the time the rope leaves
as the *tail* in your hand it carries only a whisper of what the far end drags.
The rate is the astonishing part: the hold falls not by a fixed amount per turn
but by a fixed *fraction*, so each turn strips a slice of what is left. Three
turns of hemp on an iron bitt, and one hand holds a berthing ship.

## The one catch

Purchase this cheap is paid for in control, not effort. The friction that lets
one hand hold the load is the same friction you must beat to *ease* it out:

- **Too few turns — it takes charge.** The tail overpowers your grip and the
  rope surges off the drum.
- **The working band — rendering, then holding fast.** The tail creeps under
  strain (good for lowering), then sits inside an easy grip (a make-fast).
- **Too many turns — nipped solid.** The line will not surge, but it will not
  render either; to ease it you must first throw a turn off.

The cure for a load that surges is not a stronger arm — it is another turn. The
cure for a line you cannot ease is a turn taken off.

The bench uses the capstan (belt-friction) equation directly:

```
T_hold = T_load · e^(−μθ)      θ = 2π · turns      purchase = e^(μθ)
```

Greasy rope on a smooth drum takes `μ ≈ 0.12`; hemp on iron, `0.20`; dry and
rough, `0.33`. The load's size never enters the *ratio* — only the turns and the
grip.

## The bench

One drum from above — the load comes in on the thick line, wraps the drum, and
leaves as the tail in your hand. Set the **load**, the **turns**, and the
**surface**. The page shows the hold you need on the tail, the purchase the wrap
