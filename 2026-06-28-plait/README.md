# Plait

**Three small pages, made the same morning, for the same idea seen from three sides:
strength is a few strands, ordered — not a heap of loose thread.**

Most days in this folder are one page. Some mornings the idea comes in
threes. A plait is what you make when you stop hoarding loose strands and
start disciplining a few of them: you take three, and over and over you
cross the outer one to the middle, until what was a soft heap that pulls
apart in the hand becomes a cord you can moor a boat with. The rope is not
strong because it has many fibres. A bale of carded wool has more fibres
than any rope and holds nothing. It is strong because a few of them are
ordered, crossed in turn, and made to take the load together. These three
started apart — a joint, a loom, a cellar — and they meet here, because
they are all saying the one thing in three trades.

## The three

- **[Tenon](./tenon/)** — *hold by fit, not by fastening.* A frame does
  not stand because you drove forty nails into it; nails work loose and
  the rack pulls it to a parallelogram. It stands because a few joints
  were cut to fit one mortise exactly, dry-tried, driven home, and pegged.
  A small page for the few commitments cut true enough to hold under load,
  against the many tacked on and working loose.
  Rough → Cut → Fitted → Seated. Loose → Paring → Driving → Holding.

- **[Heddle](./heddle/)** — *raise only the threads you mean to cross
  now.* You cannot lift the whole warp at once and still weave — raise
  everything and the shuttle has nowhere to pass and you get no cloth. The
  heddle lifts the chosen few, the shed opens, the pick goes through, and
  it beats home. A small page for the deliberate few you raise and cross
  this hour, against the whole anxious warp held up at once.
  Slack → Threaded → Raised → Crossed. Loose → Drawn → Picked → Beaten.

- **[Spile](./spile/)** — *draw a little, often, and keep the cask
  sound.* Broach every barrel in the cellar at once and air gets to all of
  them and the lot turns to vinegar by the weekend. Tend a few — a vent
  peg eased in, drawn slow, racked off bright — and they keep clear for
  years. A small page for the few works you actually broach and draw down,
  against the many opened all at once and left to sour.
  Bunged → Spiled → Drawing → Racked. Flat → Working → Clearing → Sound.

## The shared shape

Each page is the same small machine the folder keeps building: a hero, the
problem stated plainly, three steps, an interactive board of a dozen
seeded entries you can sort, advance, and reset, four levels and four
conditions, a handful of people who keep one of these for real, and a
quiet waitlist that promises one email and no streaks. A live gauge reads
the whole board at once and only stays calm if you keep it to a tended
few. No build step — open any `index.html` in a browser. It all lives in
your browser; nothing leaves it.
