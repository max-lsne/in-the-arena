A Sunday ritual for long work

An honest record
of long work.

You are three years into the book, or the doctorate, or the quiet company. The weeks blur. The good weeks blur with the bad ones. Cairn is the smallest possible practice: every Sunday, you place one stone for the week you had. Made. Learned. Navigated. Rested. Lost. The trail is the only record you need.

See a trail Mark a Sunday

One stone a week. No streaks. No score. No share button.

The tools you have were built for quarters.
Your work runs in years.

Notion forgets. Calendars overwrite. Productivity apps measure days. The honest record of a multi-year project is usually one sentence: I think it went well? I'm not sure anymore.

Cairn keeps the only thing worth keeping at this scale: the shape of the trail. Rest weeks and lost weeks belong on it too. A clean cairn is a lie. An honest one is a map.

The ritual

  1. 01

    Sit

    Sunday, late afternoon. Phone face down. Take three minutes. You are not solving anything — you are noticing.

  2. 02

    Choose a stone

    Made. Learned. Navigated. Rested. Lost. One per week. There is no sixth option. There is no half-week.

  3. 03

    Write a sentence

    Not a paragraph. Not a list. One sentence — the truest one you can manage about the week that just ended.

  4. 04

    Place it

    It joins the trail. You don't read the old ones. You don't share it. In six months you'll look back. The shape will surprise you.

What the stones mean

  • Made. A draft. A chapter. A working build. A page that didn't exist on Monday.
  • Learned. Reading. Conversations. The kind of week that changes what next month looks like.
  • Navigated. A decision. A turn. A direction taken, even if no page was written.
  • Rested. On purpose. Long work without rest is a story about how the work ended.
  • Lost. The week got away. Note it honestly. A trail without lost weeks isn't your trail.

Who keeps a cairn

The doctoral candidate

Year three. Two more to go. The thesis is a slow animal. Cairn is what you'll show your future self when you can't remember if you've moved at all.

The book writer

The book is eighteen months in and the middle third is still the middle third. The stones from this season will be the dedication of the next one.

The slow founder

Not raising. Not scaling. Building the thing in five years instead of eighteen months. You need a record that does not lie about that pace.

The indie game maker

Years into the build. Devlogs are for other people. The cairn is for you, on the Sundays you wonder if you should be doing something else.
