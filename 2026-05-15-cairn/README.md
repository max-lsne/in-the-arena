# Cairn

**An honest record of long work.**

Cairn is a Sunday ritual for people on multi-year work — the
dissertation, the book, the slow company, the years-long indie game.
Every Sunday at 5pm local, you get one prompt. Choose a stone. Write a
sentence. Place it. The trail builds itself.

## The pitch in one breath

Your tools were built for quarters. Your work runs in years. Cairn keeps
the only thing worth keeping at that scale: the shape of the trail.

## Five stones, one a week

- **Made** — a draft, a chapter, a working build. A page that didn't
  exist on Monday.
- **Learned** — reading, conversations. The kind of week that changes
  what next month looks like.
- **Navigated** — a decision, a turn. A direction taken, even if no
  page was written.
- **Rested** — on purpose. Long work without rest is a story about how
  the work ended.
- **Lost** — the week got away. A trail without lost weeks isn't your
  trail.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — landing page with the in-browser trail demo.
- `styles.css` — dawn-cream, granite, and moss; Source Serif 4 for
  headings, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for marginalia.
- `script.js` — twenty-eight seeded stones from a doctoral year two;
  retag, reweight, add this Sunday's stone, reset.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
