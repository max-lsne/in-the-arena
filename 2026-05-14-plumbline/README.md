# Plumbline

**Are you still building what you said you'd build?**

Plumbline is a quarterly calibration ritual for product teams. On day one
of the cycle, you write three to five things this quarter is for. As work
ships, you tag it. Mid-cycle, you sight the plumbline: stated weight
versus actual weight, with the drift angle in degrees. Re-aim — or
restate, on purpose.

## The pitch in one breath

You don't have a focus problem. You have a drift problem. The work that
landed nowhere on day forty is the cycle that surprised you on day ninety.

## The ritual

- **State** — Three to five sentences. The version you would defend in a
  board meeting, not the inspirational one.
- **Tag** — Every shipped item gets tagged to a focus. If it fits none of
  them, that's a signal, not a bug.
- **Sight** — Look at the drift mid-cycle. The plumbline is honest about
  the gap between what you said and what you did.
- **Settle** — Re-aim toward the stated focuses, or restate them with the
  picture you actually have now.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — landing page with the in-browser calibration demo.
- `styles.css` — linen, ink, and brass palette; Fraunces serif with Inter
  and JetBrains Mono.
- `script.js` — four sample focuses, twelve shipped items, a live drift
  gauge (RMSE-based angle) you can re-weight and retag.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
