A quarterly calibration ritual

Are you still building
what you said you'd build?

Every quarter starts with a clear answer. By week six, the team is busy — but on what? Plumbline is the quiet check: state what the cycle is for, tag the work as it ships, and sight the drift before it costs you the quarter.

One ritual, four times a year. No dashboards to babysit.

You don't have a focus problem.
You have a drift problem.

On day one of the quarter, every team agrees what the cycle is for. On day forty, half the work belongs to a fifth thing nobody named. By day eighty, the retrospective is an archaeological dig.

Plumbline doesn't catch drift after it's done. It shows you the angle while there's still time to re-aim — or to admit that the new heading is the right one, and restate.

The ritual

  1. 01

    State

    At the start of the cycle, write three to five things this quarter is for. One sentence each. The version you would actually defend in a board meeting.

  2. 02

    Tag

    As work ships, tag it to one of those things. If it doesn't fit any of them, that's a signal — not a bug. Make it explicit.

  3. 03

    Sight

    Mid-cycle, look at the plumbline. Stated weight versus actual weight. The drift angle in degrees. The work that landed nowhere.

  4. 04

    Settle

    At cycle end, choose. Re-aim the team toward what you said. Or restate, on purpose, with the new picture. Either is fine. Drift is not.
