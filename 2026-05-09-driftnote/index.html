From fleeting to findable, in three steps.

  1. 01

    Catch it

    One tap. Talk for as long as you want. Driftnote transcribes in the background — even offline. No editing, no formatting, no decisions.

  2. 02

    Let it weave

    Driftnote reads what's already there and finds the thread your new thought belongs on. You don't pick a folder. The folder picks you.

  3. 03

    Hear the echoes

    When a thought returns, Driftnote shows you the last time it visited. The pattern you couldn't see from inside the week becomes the prompt for next week.
